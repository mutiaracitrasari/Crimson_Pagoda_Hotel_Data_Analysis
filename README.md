# Crimson Pagoda Hotel Data Analysis

This project presents a comprehensive data analysis of hotel bookings at **Crimson Pagoda Hotel**. Using Python and popular data science libraries, the notebook explores key booking trends, guest demographics, and cancellation patterns to uncover actionable insights for business improvement.

## Project Overview

The primary objectives of this analysis are:

* To identify patterns in booking cancellations
* To examine guest distribution by country and group type
* To analyze seasonal trends in bookings
* To evaluate the popularity of room types
* To explore the impact of lead time and special requests on booking outcomes

## Notebook Structure

The notebook includes the following sections:

1. **Importing Libraries**
   Required libraries such as `pandas`, `numpy`, `matplotlib`, and `seaborn`.

2. **Loading the Dataset**
   Reads the hotel booking dataset (CSV format) and performs basic exploration.

3. **Data Cleaning**
   Handles missing values, drops irrelevant columns, and corrects inconsistencies.

4. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
   Visualization and statistical analysis of various booking features.

5. **Insights & Observations**
   Summarizes the key takeaways and implications for business strategy.

## Key Visualizations

The notebook includes visualizations such as:

* Booking cancellations by market segment
* Room type distribution
* Monthly booking trends
* Guest composition by adults, children, and babies
* Correlation heatmaps and bar charts

## Business Insights

* Cancellations tend to be higher for bookings with longer lead times and fewer special requests.
* Most bookings occur during the summer months, with August showing the highest volume.
* Guests from a small number of countries make up the majority of bookings.
* Room Type A is the most frequently booked, which may inform inventory planning and pricing strategies.
